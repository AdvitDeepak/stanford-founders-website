Events & Services

Events every week on Luma

Browse past events

  • Founder programming

    A regular speaker series and workshops for members, on fundraising, recruiting, go-to-market and other operating topics, led by founders and partners.

    • Speaker series and firesides with operators
    • Fundraising, recruiting, and go-to-market workshops
    • Office hours with partners and angels

  • Co-founder matching

    Curated dinners and introductions across the graduate schools to help members find co-founders, paired by background, sector, and stage.

    • Curated co-founder dinners
    • Profile-based introductions across schools
    • Office hours for matched pairs

  • Sector mixers

    Gatherings for members working in similar areas.

    • Bio & therapeutics
    • Climate & energy
    • Defense & deeptech
    • AI infrastructure
    • Fintech & payments

  • Investor access & Demo Day

    Partner introductions and the annual Demo Day, held each spring on campus.

    • Demo Day each spring, open to all members
    • Partner introductions at active venture firms
    • Pre-pitch reviews with alumni founders
